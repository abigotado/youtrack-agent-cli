# PR #7 review resolution

This records the disposition of the 36 comments on commit `334010c` of
[PR #7](https://github.com/abigotado/youtrack-agent-cli/pull/7).
Corrections concern proposed native protocols, documentation and the embedded
skill. They do not enable confirmation, dispatch, reconciliation, signing or
Homebrew publication. Structural documentation tests are not native Gate proof.

“Deferred optimization” means the suggested alternative was not adopted; the
existing bounded safety check remains required. Explicit first-release threat
and availability limitations are not silently removed. Full isolated inventory
materialization and real native evidence remain activation prerequisites.
